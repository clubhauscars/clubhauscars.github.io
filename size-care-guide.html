Size & Care Guide

A quick guide to choosing print sizes and caring for your photos.

Standard Print Sizes

  • 4 x 6 inches (10 x 15 cm): The most common photo size, fitting a 3:2 aspect ratio.
  • 5 x 7 inches (13 x 18 cm): Great for desktop frames and small portraits.
  • 8 x 10 inches (20 x 25 cm): Standard medium size using a 5:4 aspect ratio.
  • A4 (21 x 29.7 cm): Standard international document and art print size.

Print Care and Handling

  • Handling: Always hold prints by the outer edges with clean, dry hands to avoid oil and dirt transfer.
  • Storage: Keep unmounted prints flat inside acid-free folders or archival sleeves, away from regular cardboard or wood.
  • Display: Hang prints out of direct, harsh sunlight to prevent fading, and avoid humid areas like bathrooms.
  • Unrolling: If your print arrives rolled in a tube, take it out right away and place heavy flat books on the corners to flatten the paper safely.
