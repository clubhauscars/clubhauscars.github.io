Who runs ClubHausCars?

Hi, I'm Kalisa, the sole owner and operator of ClubHausCars. I photograph and manufacture the vast majority of the products myself as well as handling all of our order fulfillment, administrative work, media, communications, and...everything else.

In 2026, after 3 years of taking film photography, I launched the ClubHausCars print shop as a way to bring my photography to life and connect with the automotive community across Canada.

Now based in Australia, I'm excited to keep growing ClubHaus and help bring a piece of that community here too.

Thanks for being here, and welcome to the ClubHaus.
